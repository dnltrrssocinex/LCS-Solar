    Four workflows

    1. 01

      SOL | 01 | Send Solar Form

      Starts onsolar-send-form tag

      Checks the email, DND, form link and form-done tag. Sends the form email, then adds solar-form-follow-up.

      Tested
    2. 01

      SOL | 01 | Solar Form Received

      Starts onSolar - Quick Form sent

      Adds solar-form-done, takes the person out of reminders and tells my team.

      Tested
    3. 02

      SOL | 02 | Form Follow-Up

      Starts onsolar-form-follow-up tag

      Three reminders on Day 1, Day 3 and Day 7, with a rule check before each. Then a final team notice.

      Tested
    4. 02

      SOL | 02 | Stop Form Reminders

      Starts onEmail reply or solar-follow-up-stop

      If the follow-up tag is there, removes the person from reminders, removes the tag and tells my team.

      Tested
